Before You Message

A few things people usually ask.

How much does a weekend safari cost?

It depends on your dates, group size, and which conservancy you want. Message us your weekend and we will send you a straight quote. No packages to wade through.

How far in advance do I need to book?

During peak migration months vehicles fill up fast, so the earlier you message us the more dates we can offer. Sometimes we can still make a weekend work on short notice too, so just ask.

Is it really just my group in the vehicle?

Yes. Every Sundown safari is private. It is your vehicle and your guide, whatever the size of your group, and we never combine bookings with other travellers.

What's included?

Private transport from Nairobi, your guide, park and conservancy fees, and accommodation for the nights you're out. We will confirm exact inclusions once we know your dates.

The best weekends go to the people who ask first.

Weekends are limited by design, because every group gets its own vehicle and guide. Send us your dates and we will tell you honestly what is open. Asking costs nothing.
