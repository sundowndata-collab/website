Our story

We started Sundown because the best moment in Africa should belong to you

Three years of private safaris out of Nairobi taught us one thing. The magic is in the quiet, and the quiet is worth protecting.

01The moment

Twenty metres from a lion, and nobody else around

There is a moment in the Masai Mara that changes people. The sun slips below the acacia line. The air goes still. A lion breathes out twenty metres from your vehicle, and you feel it in your chest.

You will remember it for the rest of your life. That moment is the whole reason we exist.

  • ×Longer queues at sightings

The quiet that makes a sighting unforgettable was being crowded out.

02What we saw

Then the safari started to change

Year after year we watched it happen. More vehicles at every sighting. Guides spending their day managing a schedule instead of reading the bush.

Guests were travelling halfway around the world, or across Kenya, only to share their once in a lifetime moment with a bus full of strangers on a different schedule.

03What we built

So we built Sundown around one simple belief

The best wildlife moment of your life should not be shared with strangers. It should belong to you and the people you chose to bring.

That is why your vehicle and guide are never shared. Whatever the size of your group, from a couple to a big family, we plan the vehicles around you and every one of them is still yours.

04How we keep the promise

Three things we never compromise on

Your guide makes the difference

Our guides know these corridors like their own neighbourhood: which animal holds which territory, which families travel together, and where to be twenty minutes before anyone else arrives.

Places most visitors never see

We hold licences in the Mara North and Olare Motorogi conservancies, where large group tours are not permitted. Night drives, off road tracking and never more than three vehicles at a sighting.

Nothing hidden, everything checkable

We are KATO bonded and TRA licensed, and our guides carry Kenya Wildlife Service credentials. We name our guides, describe our vehicles and tell you exactly what is included before you commit.

01Is a private safari really worth the extra cost?

Ask anyone who has done both and you will hear the same thing: they wish they had gone private the first time. Your guide is watching for you and nobody else. You stay at a sighting as long as you like, and nobody is checking the clock for a bus full of strangers. For a trip you may only make once, most people find the answer is yes.

02Kenya or Tanzania, where should I go first?

Start with Kenya. The Masai Mara is one of the best places in Africa to see lions and other big cats, Nairobi is easy to reach from Europe and the US, and the famous Mara River crossings happen on the Kenya side. Tanzania is wonderful too. Many guests save it for a second trip, and most do come back for one.

03How early should I book?

For July to October, aim for 9 to 12 months ahead. For the rest of the year, 4 to 6 months is usually fine. Because we keep every vehicle small, our space on any date is limited. Park fees in Kenya went up in 2026, and we hold any price we quote for 30 days, so asking for a quote early costs you nothing and keeps your options open.

04Is Kenya safe for international travellers?

Kenya's safari regions, including the Masai Mara, Amboseli, Samburu and Laikipia, are remote wilderness areas with very few people, and hundreds of thousands of visitors travel through them safely every year. Your guide is with you throughout. As with any trip, please check your own government's latest travel advice before you go.

05When is the best time to visit the Masai Mara?

July to October is the peak of the migration, with the most action and the most visitors. January and February are dry, with excellent big cat sightings and far fewer people, and it is our personal favourite. June is green and lush, with the herds beginning to arrive at lower prices. There is no bad time, only different stories.

06What is included in the price?

Every journey includes your private 4x4 Land Cruiser with a pop up roof, a certified field guide, all park and conservancy fees, full board meals and transfers. Not included are international flights, travel insurance, tips, and extras such as balloon flights unless your journey already includes one. No hidden costs and no surprises.

07Who else will be in my vehicle?

Nobody but your own group. We plan the vehicles around your group, so everyone has a clear view and every camera has room. Big family or group of friends? We plan the vehicles and guides around you, so nobody is squeezed and every vehicle is still private to you.

08Can you build a trip around us?

Yes, and most of our guests do. The nine journeys are starting points, not fixed packages. Tell us your dates, who is coming, what you love and what you would like to spend. A honeymoon with a beach extension, a family that needs a gentler pace, a photographer who wants six hours at one sighting, all of it is normal for us. You will have a draft itinerary within 24 hours.

09Have Kenya park fees really gone up?

Yes. Kenya Wildlife Service raised park fees in 2026, and that affects every operator. The simple way to protect yourself is to ask for a quote early. We honour any price we quote for 30 days, so you can decide without pressure.

10How does booking work?

It is simple. Send us your dates, group size and rough budget through the form or on WhatsApp. We reply within 2 hours during field hours and send a draft itinerary and price within 24 hours. If you love it, a 50 percent deposit confirms your booking and locks in the price, with the balance due 60 days before you travel. Until then there is no obligation.
