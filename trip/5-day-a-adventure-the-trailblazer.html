Safari overview

Picture it

Most safaris only show you one Kenya. The Trailblazer shows you two. You begin in Samburu, where you can see reticulated giraffe, Grevy's zebra and Somali ostrich, animals you will not find in the Mara. Then you fly south for the Mara's lions, leopards and cheetahs.

Our guides know both landscapes well, and we handle every transfer between them, so you never have to think about logistics. All you have to do is look out of the window.

Day by day

Your itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Nairobi to Samburu

    Morning flight to Samburu. Afternoon arrival game drive. Briefing on Samburu's unique northern species.

  2. Day 2

    Full Day Samburu

    Dawn departure. Full day tracking Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, leopard, and Beisa oryx across dry riverbed corridors.

  3. Day 3

    Samburu to Masai Mara

    Morning game drive in Samburu. Midday transfer flight south. Check in and afternoon drive in new ecosystem.

  4. Day 4

    Full Day Masai Mara

    Full day big cat focus, lion, leopard, cheetah. River crossing positioning if migration season.

  5. Day 5

    Morning Drive and Departure

    Final dawn drive. Breakfast. Return to Nairobi.
