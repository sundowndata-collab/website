Safari overview

Picture it

Imagine lying under a sky with no city light in it. The Milky Way stretches from one horizon to the other. An armed ranger keeps watch nearby, and the sounds of the wilderness are all around you. It is a night that people remember for the rest of their lives.

The other six days bring our fullest game drive programme, with big cats, the Mara Triangle, a private conservancy and evening sky talks. Many of our guests choose it for honeymoons and milestone celebrations.
