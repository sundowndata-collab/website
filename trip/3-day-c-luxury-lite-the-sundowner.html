Safari overview

Picture it

Most of the journey to the Mara is spent on the road. On The Sundowner you skip it. You fly straight into the western Mara, and within hours you are in the middle of it all, rested and ready.

You stay at a premium tented camp with en suite facilities and attentive service, and each evening ends with a sundowner at a private lookout. The wildlife access is the same as our adventure journeys. The comfort is a step above.

Day by day

Your itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Fly In and Sunset Champagne

    Morning flight from Wilson Airport to Mara North airstrip. Transferred to luxury camp. Welcome lunch. Afternoon game drive. Signature sundowner champagne session at private lookout.

  2. Day 2

    Full Day Private Game Drive

    Guided full day on private conservancy. Morning and afternoon game drive sessions. Gourmet bush lunch in the field.

  3. Day 3

    Morning Drive and Fly Out

    Dawn game drive. Champagne breakfast at camp. Fly back to Nairobi by midday.
