Kenya vs Tanzania to Where's Best to See the Great Migration?

Published: August 20, 2025, Sundown Safaris Field Team

One Wonder, Two Countries

The Great Migrationover 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles moving across the Serengeti and Masai Mara, is often called the "greatest wildlife show on earth." But if it spans both Tanzania and Kenyawhere is the best country to witness it?

The answer depends on timing, budget, crowd tolerance, and personal preference. This guide compares Kenya vs Tanzania so you can choose the best safari for your migration experience.

1. Tanzania: Serengeti's Year Round Drama

  • Why Tanzania?
    • The migration spends about 9 months in Tanzaniamoving across the Serengeti plains.
    • Calving season (Jan to March): Over 500,000 wildebeest calves are born in Ndutu, predators are everywhere.
    • Huge landscapes means fewer crowds compared to Mara.
  • Best Times: December to July.
  • Pros:
    • Longer migration window.
    • Vast, untouched wilderness.
    • Strong predator action during calving.
  • Cons:
    • Longer travel distances inside Serengeti.
    • Higher park fees than Kenya in some zones.

2. Kenya: Masai Mara's Iconic River Crossings

  • Why Kenya?
    • From July to Octoberthe herds flood into the Masai Mara.
    • The Mara River crossingswildebeest plunging into crocodile infested waters, are migration's most dramatic spectacle.
    • Compact park means higher game density and shorter drives.
  • Pros:
    • Easier access from Nairobi (short flights or 4 to 5 hr drive).
    • Perfect for short safaris (3 to 5 nights).
    • Peak predator sightings.
  • Cons:
    • Smaller than Serengeti more concentrated tourist numbers.
    • Peak season rates (July to Oct) can be steep.

4. Cost Comparison

  • Kenya: More budget options, easier logistics (2 to 3 nights in Mara possible).
  • Tanzania: More remote, but offers exclusive experiences, ideal for longer safaris.

5. The Verdict

  • Choose Tanzania if you want calving season drama, fewer crowds, and extended migration months.
  • Choose Kenya if you want iconic river crossings, high predator action, and easier, shorter trips.
  • Best Safari Strategy: Combine both for the ultimate migration circuit.

Short Takeaway:

Kenya gives you the migration's "high drama." Tanzania gives you its "long story."
